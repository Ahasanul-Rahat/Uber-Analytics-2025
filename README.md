# Uber-Analytics-2025
This repository presents a comprehensive business analysis of Uber, a leading public technology-driven transportation company. It highlights and evaluates Uber’s business performance during the period 2024–2025.

The dashboard focuses on answering practical business questions such as:

• How is revenue and trip performance changing YoY?

• Which regions are driving growth?

• What are the major cost and profitability drivers?

• How efficiently is incentive spending supporting business growth?

• Where are the key performance gaps?
